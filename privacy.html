Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Overview

Virtus is a small web design and IT services business operated by Ruben Romero in Odessa, Texas. This policy explains how Virtus collects, uses, protects, and shares information provided through its website, customer support calls, email, and SMS communications.

Information we collect

Virtus may collect your name, business name, email address, phone number, service address, details about your technology or support request, and records of communications with you. Website hosting providers may also collect basic technical data such as browser type, device type, and IP address through standard server logs.

How information is used

Virtus uses information to respond to inquiries, provide web design and IT services, manage projects and support requests, send service related updates, schedule work, prepare quotes, maintain business records, and communicate with customers who have consented to receive SMS messages.

SMS data and consent

Phone numbers collected for SMS communications are used only for outreach and support related to Virtus web design, app development, and IT services. SMS consent is not a condition of purchase. Virtus honors SMS opt out requests immediately. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Virtus does not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or SMS consent information with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers and disclosure

Virtus may share information with service providers only as needed to operate the business and provide requested services. This includes Twilio, which provides SMS delivery, and website, email, or technical service providers that process information on behalf of Virtus. These providers receive only the information needed to perform their services.

Virtus may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or to investigate fraud or misuse.

Data security and retention

Virtus uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect customer information. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed to provide services, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting Virtus. Certain records may be retained when required for legal, security, or business record purposes. You may stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

Policy updates

Virtus may update this policy as services or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will remain available on this page.
