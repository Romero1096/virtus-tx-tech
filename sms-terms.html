Program purpose

Virtus sends SMS messages about customer inquiries, IT support requests, appointment scheduling, project status, service coordination, and other communications related to requested web design, app development, or IT services. Virtus does not use this program to send third party marketing.

How customers opt in

Customers opt in through verbal consent gathered during customer support calls. A Virtus representative asks:

“Do you agree to receive SMS messages from Virtus about your IT support requests? Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.”

Customers must clearly agree before Virtus sends SMS messages. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any service.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on the number and status of your support requests, appointments, and active projects. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt out and help

  • Reply STOP to opt out of future SMS messages from Virtus. Opt out requests are honored immediately.
  • Reply HELP for help, email virtustxtech@gmail.com, or call (432) 287-8626.

After you reply STOP, Virtus may send one final confirmation message. You will receive no further SMS messages unless you provide consent again.
